About

Frontend Simplified is a community of people who are passionate about learning frontend development. We believe that anyone can learn to code, and we are here to help you on your journey.

We offer a variety of resources to help you learn, including tutorials, articles, and a supportive community. Whether you are a complete beginner or an experienced developer, we have something for you.
